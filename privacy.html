Privacy policy

Controller: Nikita Tsymbal, ari@tsym.nl.

Visitor statistics

We count page views with Umami on our own server. It records the page, the page that linked to it, your browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language, and the country, region and city derived from your IP address. A visitor code derived from your IP address and browser changes every month; the statistics contain no IP address, nothing is stored on your device, and we share them with no one. Our server's access log records each request with your IP address and is overwritten as it fills up. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing which pages are read (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Where

The site is delivered by GitHub Pages, GitHub, Inc. (USA), which processes request data such as your IP address to serve it. Visitor statistics are stored on our own server at netcup GmbH in Nuremberg, Germany.

How long

Visitor statistics: as long as the site exists.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority.
